Strategies & Frameworks in Use

Feedback & Coaching

  • Subject-Behaviour-Impact (SBI) Framework: Core structure for difficult conversations. Most effective when prepared in advance
  • Behavioural Scales: Defining excellence, adequacy, and inadequacy for key behaviours
  • Professional Scepticism Questions: Key questions for evaluating proposals and business cases

Decision-Making

  • Multiple Perspectives: Warrior, lover, dreamer lenses for balanced assessment
  • Separation of Optimism from Judgement: Acknowledging natural optimism whilst maintaining rigorous evaluation
  • Critical Incident Protocol: Structured approach for urgent, complex matters (governance, legal, ethical, athlete wellbeing, procedure)

Personal Development

  • Pause & Prepare: Recognising anxiety signals and stepping away rather than "tap dancing" through difficult conversations
  • Journaling: Systematic reflection and record-keeping to track patterns and progress
  • AI-Enhanced Coaching: Session analysis and continuous development support between coaching sessions

Organisational Development

  • Kaizen Improvement Model: Stepwise improvement with consolidation periods rather than constant crisis response
  • "Slow to Go Fast": Investment in relationship capital and process to enable sustainable performance

Key Learnings & Principles

"You get power by using it. This doesn't mean being authoritarian—it means being clear, direct, and appropriately demanding when the situation requires it." — Michael Smith

Core Insights

  • The Agreeableness Trap: Prioritising harmony over clarity often serves neither individuals nor organisation
  • Professional Scepticism ≠ Cynicism: Question quality of thinking, not character or intent
  • Preparation Enables Courage: Structure and thoughtful preparation consistently produce better outcomes than improvisation
  • Feedback is Development: Helping people understand gaps between current and desired performance is a gift, not criticism
  • Role Clarity Matters: CEO role is ensuring rigorous decision-making, not mastering every technical domain
  • Pattern Recognition: Same underlying issues (withdrawal, over-preparation, agreeableness) manifest across different situations

Courage Strategies (from HBR Article)

  • Create Positive Narrative: Risk hunting, moral quest, calling on faith/purpose
  • Cultivate Confidence: Train deliberately, assemble large tool kit, focus on what's controllable
  • Take Small Steps: Evaluate objectively, let meaning emerge, interpret in motion
  • Find Connection: Turn to allies, access resources, accept feedback
  • Stay Calm: Practice self-care, embrace rituals, reframe situations
